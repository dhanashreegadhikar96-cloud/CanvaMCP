# Canva connector: what you can ask

Example prompts for the Canva chat, grouped by what Canva's MCP tools can do. Swap in your own names, text and
designs. You can refer to designs by name ("my Diwali post"), by link, or by "the one you just made".

Based on the 48 tools Canva's MCP server gave this app (7 Oct 2026, from `.canva-debug.log`) and the operations of
Canva's design editor tool. The live site uses Canva's Developer Portal MCP server, whose list may differ slightly;
if a prompt fails, the step's **Details** show Canva's reason.

**Good to know**
- **Creating with Canva AI** uses your Canva AI allowance and a per-minute limit. Editing, exporting, searching and
  organising don't.
- **Edits are saved only after you say yes** in the chat. You can ask for changes before saving, or cancel.
- **Plan features:** resizing, brand kits, brand templates and autofill need Canva Pro / Teams or above. Premium
  elements export clean only on paid plans.
- One request at a time works best: "change the title, then make it gold" is fine; ten unrelated changes in one
  message are harder to check.

## 1. Create new designs (Canva AI)

- "Create an Instagram post for our Diwali sale, festive and elegant, with the text 'Festive Edit – 20% off'."
- "Make a 6-slide pitch deck for <client> about audit automation, modern blue and white."
- "Create a poster announcing the team offsite on 14 November in Goa."
- "Design a LinkedIn banner for our product launch."
- "Make an A4 flyer for the new leave policy with these points: …"
- "Create a presentation about Q3 results using this outline: 1) … 2) … 3) …"
- "Create a doc summarising these meeting notes: …"
- "Show me a few options for a Diwali post and let me pick one." (Canva offers candidate designs to choose from)

Formats you can name: Instagram post / story, Facebook post, LinkedIn post / banner, YouTube thumbnail, presentation,
poster, flyer, A4 document, doc, whiteboard, email, or a custom size ("1200 × 628 px").

## 2. Images

- "Generate an image of a diya on a dark blue patterned background."
- "Generate a photo-style image of a team working in a bright office, for a slide."
- "Remove the background from the photo on page 1 of <design>." (Pro features may apply)
- "Separate the layers of the image on page 2 so I can edit its parts."
- "Upload this image into my Canva: <public image link>."
- "Replace the main photo on page 1 with <uploaded image>."
- "Add <uploaded image> to page 2, top right, 400 px wide."

## 3. Edit text

- "In <design>, change 'Celebrate in Style' to 'Shine This Diwali'."
- "Replace every 'Q3' with 'Q4' in <presentation>."
- "Rewrite the headline on slide 2 to be shorter."
- "Add a text box at the bottom of page 1 saying 'Offer valid till 30 Nov'."
- "Rename the design to 'Diwali Post – Final'."
- "Fix the spelling mistakes on slide 3."

## 4. Format text

- "Make the title bigger (72 px) and bold."
- "Make 'Diwali Edit 2026' gold (#D4AF37)."
- "Italicise the subtitle and centre it."
- "Underline the call to action and link it to https://example.com."
- "Turn the points on slide 3 into a bulleted list."
- "Increase the line spacing of the paragraph on page 2."
- Font family: may not be possible with the editor tools (unconfirmed). If not, the chat will say so and offer size,
  weight or colour instead.

## 5. Move, resize and style elements

- "Move the logo to the top right corner."
- "Make the photo on page 1 smaller and move it to the left."
- "Delete the small icon at the bottom."
- "Recolour the background shape to navy (#1E3A8A)."
- "Make the overlay 50% transparent."
- "Bring the text to the front."
- "Rotate the badge 15 degrees."
- "Group the title and subtitle together."
- "Flip the photo horizontally."
- "Crop the photo on slide 2 to a square."
- "Change the outline of the box to 2 px dark grey."

## 6. Shapes, arrows and diagrams

- "Create a slide showing our approval process as boxes connected by arrows: Request → Review → Approve → Archive."
- "Draw the Super Admin and End User flow as two rows of boxes with arrows: …"
- "Add a rounded blue box with the text 'Step 1' on the left of slide 3."
- "Add an arrow from the 'Review' box to the 'Approve' box."
- "Change the arrow ends to triangles."
- "Make all boxes on this slide the same size and line them up."

Tip: give the steps in order and say how you want them laid out (left to right, top to bottom, two rows).

## 7. Pages and slides

- "Add a new slide after slide 3 with the title 'Pricing'."
- "Move slide 5 before slide 2."
- "Delete page 4." (the chat asks you to confirm)
- "Combine the slides from <deck A> and <deck B> into one presentation."
- "Make a copy of <design> called '<name> v2'."
- "Add speaker notes to slide 1: 'Introduce the team and agenda.'"
- "What are the speaker notes on slide 3?"

## 8. Resize and reformat (Pro / Teams)

- "Resize the Diwali post into an Instagram story."
- "Make a LinkedIn version of this poster."
- "Turn this presentation into an A4 document."

## 9. Find and read designs

- "Show my 10 most recently edited designs."
- "Search my designs for 'offsite'."
- "Find designs shared with me about the Q3 report."
- "What's in my 'Team Offsite' presentation? Summarise each slide."
- "How many pages does <design> have?"
- "Show me a preview of page 2." (the chat describes it; previews open in Canva)
- "Open this Canva link: https://canva.link/…" (short links are resolved)

## 10. Organise folders

- "Create a folder called 'Q4 Campaign'."
- "Move the Diwali posts into 'Q4 Campaign'."
- "What's in my 'Marketing' folder?"
- "Find my folder called 'Clients'."

## 11. Export and download

- "Export <design> as PDF."
- "Export page 1 of the Diwali post as PNG."
- "What formats can I export this design in?"
- "Export the deck as PowerPoint (PPTX)."
- "Export the animation as MP4 / GIF." (for designs with motion)

Download links expire after 24 hours. Free plans get standard quality; premium elements may block clean export.

## 12. Import existing files

- "Import this PowerPoint into Canva: <public link to .pptx>."
- "Turn this PDF into an editable Canva design: <public link>."

The file must be reachable at a public link.

## 13. Comments and review

- "Add a comment on <design>: 'Please check the numbers on slide 4.'"
- "Show the comments on the offsite poster."
- "Reply to the latest comment: 'Fixed, thanks!'"

## 14. Brand kits and templates (Pro / Teams / Enterprise)

- "Which brand kits do I have?"
- "Create a product launch post using our brand kit." (on-brand generation)
- "Search our brand templates for 'event poster'."
- "Create a design from the 'Event poster' brand template with title 'Offsite 2026' and date '14 Nov'."
- "Which fields can be filled in the 'Weekly report' template?"
- "Save this design as a new brand template draft." / "Publish the brand template."

## 15. Data fields (autofill)

- "Which text and image fields in <design> can be filled automatically?"
- "Fill the template's fields with: name 'Asha', role 'Lead Auditor', date '14 Nov'."
- "Mark the title text as an autofill field called 'headline'."

## 16. Help

- "What can you do in Canva?"
- "How do I add my logo to a design in Canva?" (answers from Canva's help)

## What it can't do (yet)

- Delete whole designs or folders.
- Read or use files from other NSOffice connectors on its own (paste the content instead).
- Edit designs other people own unless they're shared with you with edit access.
- Change the font family, unless Canva's tools support it (unconfirmed).
- Get around Canva's limits: AI allowance, per-minute limit, plan features.
