# Canva connector: business use cases

How NSOffice users can use the Canva connector: they describe what they need in chat, and it's done in **their own
Canva account** as real, editable designs. Each use case lists who it's for, example prompts, and what to watch for
(plan limits, usage allowance).

**What makes it useful**
- No switching tools: work from the NSOffice chat, get a Canva link back.
- Results are normal Canva designs with separate elements, so teams can keep refining them in Canva.
- Works within each user's own Canva plan, brand kit and permissions.

**Things that apply to every use case**
- Creating designs with Canva AI uses the user's **AI allowance** and a **per-minute limit**; editing, exporting and
  organising don't. Canva Pro / Teams have larger allowances.
- Premium elements, brand kits, brand templates and resizing depend on the user's plan.
- Edits are saved only after the user confirms in chat.

## 1. Marketing: social posts and campaign assets

- **Who:** marketing and social media teams.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Create a Diwali Instagram post for our ethnic menswear brand, festive but elegant."
  - "Make the same post as an Instagram story." (resize)
  - "Change the headline to 'Festive Edit – 20% off' and export as PNG."
- **Value:** first drafts in minutes; quick variants per channel; consistent edits without reopening the editor.
- **Watch for:** AI allowance on heavy campaign days; premium photos may need Pro to export clean.

## 2. Sales: pitch decks and proposals

- **Who:** sales and account managers.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Make a 6-slide pitch deck for <client> about our audit automation, modern blue style."
  - "Add a slide after slide 3 with these pricing points: …"
  - "Export the deck as PDF so I can attach it."
- **Value:** tailored decks per prospect without starting from scratch; fast last-minute changes before meetings.

## 3. Internal communications and HR

- **Who:** HR, internal comms, office managers.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Create a poster announcing the team offsite on 14 November in Goa."
  - "Make an A4 flyer for the new leave policy with these three points: …"
  - "Change the date on the offsite poster to 21 November."
- **Value:** non-designers produce on-brand notices; updates are a one-line request.

## 4. Process and training material (diagrams)

- **Who:** product, operations, training, consulting teams.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Create a slide showing the Super Admin and End User flow as boxes connected by arrows: …"
  - "Turn these onboarding steps into a one-page visual guide."
- **Value:** process flows built from editable boxes, text and arrows, ready for workshops and documentation.
- **Watch for:** complex diagrams may need small manual layout tweaks in Canva.

## 5. Reports to presentations (e.g. AuditBee outputs)

- **Who:** audit, compliance and analytics teams in NSOffice.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Create a 5-slide summary of these audit findings for the client meeting: …"
  - "Make a one-page executive summary doc with these key numbers."
- **Value:** turns findings already in NSOffice into client-ready slides; pairs naturally with other NSOffice Bees.
- **Watch for:** the user pastes or provides the content; the connector doesn't read other NSOffice data on its own yet.

## 6. Design library management

- **Who:** anyone with a large Canva workspace; design ops.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Show my 10 most recently edited designs."
  - "Create a folder 'Q4 Campaign' and move the Diwali posts into it."
  - "Find the presentation about the offsite and export it as PDF."
- **Value:** find, organise and export without browsing Canva; no AI allowance used.

## 7. Quick updates and re-exports

- **Who:** anyone maintaining recurring material (price sheets, event posters, menus).
- **Prompts:**
  - "In the price sheet, change 'Starter' to ₹999 and export as PDF."
  - "Update the event date on all three posters." (one at a time)
- **Value:** small, frequent changes take seconds; saves designer time for new work.

## 8. Imports and repurposing

- **Who:** teams with existing PowerPoint, PDF or Word material.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Import this deck into Canva: <public link to .pptx>."
  - "Resize the imported deck for a LinkedIn carousel."
- **Value:** brings legacy material into Canva for refreshing and reuse.
- **Watch for:** the file must be at a publicly reachable link.

## 9. Brand-consistent content at scale (Pro / Teams)

- **Who:** brand and marketing teams with a Canva brand kit or brand templates.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Create a product launch post using our brand kit."
  - "Make a new version of our brand template with these details: …"
- **Value:** on-brand output by default; fewer review cycles.
- **Watch for:** needs a Canva plan with brand kits / brand templates.

## 10. Collaboration and review

- **Who:** teams reviewing designs together.
- **Prompts:**
  - "Add a comment on the pitch deck: 'Please check the numbers on slide 4.'"
  - "Show the comments on the offsite poster."
- **Value:** feedback captured in Canva without leaving NSOffice.
